# Privacy

This site presents our reports and offers free samples. There are no accounts, and the site does not collect or store personal data.

## Purchases

Reports are sold through Kyobo Book Centre. Your name, contact details and payment information are collected and processed by Kyobo under its own privacy policy; LIMEN RESEARCH does not receive them.

## Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and delete them within a year once the matter is closed.

## Server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep access logs (such as IP addresses) for security under its own privacy policy. We use no analytics or advertising cookies.
